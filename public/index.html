      CITY BLOCK

      Top-down city shootout. A new map every game, weapons on the streets, cars to steal and walls to blow up.

      You're invited to room

      or join with a room code

      How to play
      • Computer: WASD - move, Shift - sprint, mouse - aim, left button - shoot (hold for full auto), 1-9, 0 or the wheel - weapons (in toolbar order), F - get in and out of cars, H - horn, V - zoom in or out, T - mic in voice chat, M - map, Tab - scoreboard.
      • In a car: W/S - gas and brake, A/D - steer, Space - handbrake (brake and drift), mouse - shoot out of the window, Q/E - radio (80s, 90s and 2000s hits, eurodance, synthwave, rock, or off).
      • Phone: left thumb - walk and steer, right thumb - aim and shoot (hold to keep firing, a quick tap fires once). Buttons on the right: sprint, get in a car, horn, radio.
      • Weapons and ammo show up on the map about every 15 seconds, and crates hold even more. Crates come back.
      • Mushroom: +50 health and 10 seconds of acid colors. While it lasts, you never get tired, you can shoot while sprinting and you take half the damage.
      • Money: $100 for a player, $50 for a cop, $80 for SWAT and army, $300 for a helicopter; pedestrians sometimes drop a wad of cash. When you die, half your money drops on the ground. To spend it, step onto a circle: the hospital heals you on the spot, +10 health for $5; at the gun shop you pick a weapon at the top of the screen (pistol $50 ... minigun $1000), and buying one you already have refills its ammo; at the garage, in a car, you get a respray, a repair and a clean record ($1000).
      • Everything breaks: glass, fences, crates, trees, brick walls. Barrels and cars explode. Wrecked buildings rebuild themselves brick by brick after a minute without gunfire.
      • Cars with drivers use the roads - don't run out in front of them.
      • Motorcycles are parked by the hospital, the gun shop and in parking lots: faster and nimbler than cars, but the rider is exposed - bullets, fire and blasts can hit you.
      • The more wanted stars, the bigger the chase: 3 - SWAT, 4 - a helicopter (it doesn't shoot but drops SWAT; aim at it and your bullets fly up), 5 - tanks (they don't shoot, they crush). You can steal the SWAT truck and the tank: in the tank, attack fires the cannon.
      • Walk into a building and its roof turns see-through. Nobody outside can see who's inside.
      • Modes are picked when you create a room and travel to your friends with the link. "Free for all" - everyone for themselves. "Gang" - the whole room is on one side: you can't hurt each other at all (bullets pass through your own), the wanted level is shared - one of you messes up and all of you get hunted, and the stars only go out once everyone is dead; a fallen member respawns next to the others. Your crew is labeled in green.
